Four precise findings

  • Inner-product boundary: J² = −I alone does not force Euclidean norm preservation. This does not refute a theorem that separately assumes skew-adjointness.
  • Incorrect unrestricted shortcut: two skew-adjoint factors need not have a self-adjoint product.
  • Vacuous field: a sheaf “symmetry” condition ends in “or True”.
  • Incorrect sum: clamped Hodge indices add out-of-range entries to a diagonal sum.
