# Evidence limits and rejected branches

- This audit was requested after the source existed. `PLAN.md` was committed
  after initial source inspection and before execution; it is not a blinded
  preregistration. F03/F04 were exploratory findings from subsequent inspection.
- No 102-equation or FieldSpace embedding was imposed on this independent Lean
  project. Shared mathematical words are insufficient to justify such a mapping.
- The full historical Zoo roster is 46. Five extensions are separately recorded;
  this does not turn the historical roster into 51 canonical animals.
- All 46 Python selected adapters executed their published conformance examples.
  The examples are synthetic fixtures, not measurements from Ramtastic, Hypernym,
  Matthew, or any human interaction.
- Only WEASEL and ELEPHANT received target-specific inputs. WEASEL filters four
  already supplied counterexamples. Its severity/reproducibility values are
  Boolean execution indicators, and cost counts changed fixture keys. They are
  not calibrated risk, probability, or cost estimates. ELEPHANT contains one
  documented snapshot; “origin” denotes the boundary of that input, not the first
  historical event in the repository.
- CIRCUS validates a report gate with material findings; MONSTER returns N/A
  because the performance-envelope eligibility requirements are absent. Neither
  output establishes the truth of the declarations supplied to it.
- Ninety fixture comparisons and 22 engine tests share upstream specifications.
  They establish selected software regression behavior, not independent science.
- Python source-certificate generators for the five extensions executed; their
  generated Lean certificate files were not rebuilt as part of this target audit.
  Prior upstream compilation evidence is not presented as a new local result.
- The original build failure is retained. A shallow-checkout failure in an audit
  diagnostic is an audit-harness error, not a Ramtastic mathematical defect. The
  namespace-only attempt then exposed two remaining zero-form goals; its failure
  is also retained. A second proof attempt rewrote the zero form before its
  projection lemma could match and failed; that log is retained too. The final
  diagnostic explicitly unfolds the projections and pullback.
- The first compiled audit harness used a private Lean API that changed in the
  pinned toolchain, and its quaternion probe initially exceeded definitional
  reduction. Those are audit-code failures, not defects in the target. The
  corrected harness uses the public `Lean.collectAxioms` API; the matrix proof
  checks each finite entry explicitly. A later run passed both declaration scans
  and correctly rejected native proof evaluation, but the control expected an
  older axiom name. Its remaining matrix-coordinate reduction and redundant
  tactic were then corrected. Failed logs remain in the package; their
  error-recovery `sorryAx` output belongs to failed audit code, not to the target.
- The local environment had only a different Lean version and an incomplete
  Mathlib cache. An initial standalone probe attempt failed on missing
  `Mathlib/Tactic.olean`. No local Lean acceptance is claimed from that attempt;
  exact-toolchain Lean evidence comes from the linked hosted runs.
- No `sorry`/custom-axiom token was found by the comment/string-stripping lexical
  scan. Error-recovery placeholders printed after an unresolved identifier do not
  mean the original author wrote `sorry`. Only the compiled dependency audit can
  establish the relevant axiom property of a successfully elaborated environment.
- The four witnesses refute unrestricted readings or formula identities; they
  are not counterexamples to the Lean kernel or to correctly stated conditional
  theorems. In particular F01 does not refute the separately assumed skew-adjoint
  Clifford-action version of Bass (i).
- The private Vega code, halting construction, product stack and benchmark data
  were not supplied. No conclusion about them, or about the author's intent, is
  justified by this audit.
